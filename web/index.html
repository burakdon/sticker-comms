How it works

Each sticker was sent by someone in a chat. Your job is to guess what the sender meant:

  • Positive: happy, supportive, excited, affectionate.
  • Negative: sad, annoyed, disappointed, tired.
  • Sarcastic: joking, teasing, or saying the opposite of what they mean.

Sometimes you will see the message the sticker replied to. Sometimes the sticker is hidden and you only see the message. Answer anyway, with your best guess.

After each answer, say how sure you were. Try to answer quickly.
